# Among Us 3D/VR Weeping Angel

A mod for Among Us 3D/VR that adds a Weeping Angel to the game.

Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/4ssH3ZwCJb) for early updates, help with my mods, and future mods.

The angel only moves when nobody is looking at it. As long as at least one player is looking at it, it stays frozen. Look away and it comes for whoever is closest. And if it touches you, you die.

Stare at it for too long and it gets impatient. After 45 seconds a countdown from 5 shows up above its head, and once that hits zero it starts moving even while you're watching. It starts slow and speeds up, so you need to get out of sight quickly.

## How a round works

- No impostors get picked. The angel is the only threat.
- Lights stay off the whole round.
- Meetings and body reports are disabled.
- The angel gets a little faster the longer the round goes.
- Crew wins by finishing tasks. The angel wins if it kills everyone.

## Install

1. Install [MelonLoader](https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader) on Among Us 3D or Among Us VR.
2. Drop `AU3DWeepingAngel.dll` into the game's `Mods` folder.
3. Launch the game.

Everyone in the lobby needs the mod installed. So players without the mod won't see it.

## Notes

- It only works in private or modded lobbies via Utilus.
- Everyone needs the mod, and you can make a modded lobby with Utilus
